Sources & method

The columns share a depth scale starting at each well’s ground surface. Move through the detailed tables for the recorded interval descriptions. Hatching marks a depth without a mapped interval. Different ground elevations and the distances between wells make these spot records unsuitable for tracing a continuous formation.

The first selection covers 18 Langley well tags. The Nova Scotia sample uses eight logs per community across six communities in Halifax County from the province’s 2020 public database. Both sources report intervals in feet. Metres use 0.3048 metres per foot.
